About

Emergency Health Aid is an educational first-aid resource. It gives basic first-aid steps for common emergencies and points you to professional help. It does not diagnose or treat conditions and must never delay calling emergency services.

Content is based on general guidance from the Indian Red Cross Society and American Red Cross and should be reviewed by a trained medical or first-aid professional. No personal data is collected or stored.
